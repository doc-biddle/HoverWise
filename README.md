# HoverWise

<img src="media/logo.png" alt="HoverWise logo" width="128" align="right">

A small WoW Forever addon that makes tooltips more useful.

- Tooltips follow the cursor, in the game world and (optionally) the interface.
- Unit tooltips show what the unit is targeting, what that target is targeting, and who in your group targets it.
- Class colours, a difficulty-coloured level line, health numbers and your faction standing.
- Members of your guild show their guild rank.
- Ore veins, herbs, beasts, recipes and lockboxes show the profession level they need, such as "Mining 175", coloured against your own skill.
- Tooltip size and background opacity.

## Settings

Options > AddOns > HoverWise, or type `/hoverwise`. The settings can also be found with the options search box.

## Installing

Install it from the WowUp Hub, or download the zip from Releases and extract the `HoverWise` folder into `_forever_\Interface\AddOns`.
